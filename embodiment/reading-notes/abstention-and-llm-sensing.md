## What is new in Gravity from Touch (narrowed again, 2026-10-25)

Not new: language models reading raw sensor data (Penetrative AI, HARGPT); touch plus language models (Octopi); language models failing to abstain (SelfAware, AbstentionBench); scoring accuracy and abstention together on labeled items (AbstentionBench); reasoning going with worse abstention (AbstentionBench, by manipulation; Liu et al. 2025); an instruction to abstain helping without fixing it (AbstentionBench §4.4, RoboAbstention §4.4); embodied abstention with rule-derived labels, including questions a missing sense cannot settle, and more reasoning effort lowering it (RoboAbstention, PARTIAL); paired answerable/unanswerable items regenerated to resist contamination (AgentAbstain, abstract).

Possibly new, on this evidence (two PARTIAL reads and abstracts): the combination of **raw numeric body-sensor readings** (touch and an accelerometer) about **the model's own physical state**, where answerability is **fixed by a physical law** (the equivalence principle) in a simulator we built, so that one set of readings, from senses the body does have, carries both answerable and unanswerable questions, with labels true by construction rather than derived from a model's perception; scored by code, not a judge; on fresh seeded items kept private until the run; shipped as a replayable bench.

Labels for the report's findings: over-claiming = adaptation (AbstentionBench; RoboAbstention, whose missing-capability category is the nearest analogue); direction accuracy = adaptation (Penetrative AI, HARGPT); instruction does not stop over-claiming = consistent with AbstentionBench §4.4 and RoboAbstention §4.4 (we have no no-instruction condition, so not a replication); H3 thinking association = adaptation of AbstentionBench §4.3 and RoboAbstention §4.2, weaker (observational, vanishes with the item fixed); H2, the failed replication and the spread study = bench checks, new for this bench only; all direction misses one error type = new (exploratory).
